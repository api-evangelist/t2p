# T2P

> T2P Co., Ltd. is a Bangkok, Thailand fintech (founded 2011) offering comprehensive payment solutions for enterprises: a white-label digital wallet platform, payment gateway, corporate card issuance, spend management, business escrow, and the DeepPocket consumer e-wallet. T2P has no public developer/API documentation surface as of this writing; integration is arranged directly with the company.

## About
- Company: T2P Co., Ltd. (DeepPocket)
- Headquarters: Bangkok, Thailand
- Founded: 2011
- Backing: 500 Global (500 Startups / 500 TukTuks), Benchachinda Holding, DTAC Accelerate, J Ventures
- Scale (self-reported): 10M+ customers, 30+ corporate clients

## Products
- Payment Gateway — accept cards, mobile wallets, and in-store payments
- White-label Wallet — enterprise wallet platform with transaction management and business insights
- Card Issuance — corporate/prepaid card programs
- Spend Management — real-time card usage monitoring and spending controls
- Escrow for Business — transaction-security / escrow services
- DeepBlok — loyalty and customer-engagement program
- DeepPocket — consumer e-wallet mobile app (top-ups, payments, global transfers; Wise integration for remittance)
- T2P Flex — additional financial solutions
